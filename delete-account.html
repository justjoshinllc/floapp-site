Delete your Flo account

Flo Forecast is made by JustJoshin LLC. You can delete your Flo account, and everything Flo holds about you, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Flo and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account at the bottom.
  3. Confirm with Delete everything.

If you subscribe on the web, Flo asks you to cancel that subscription first, so your card isn't charged for an account that's gone. A subscription through Apple or Google Play is separate: deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

Without the app

Email support@floapp.app from the email address on your account, with "Delete my account" in the subject. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, say so and we'll find your account with you.

What's deleted

  • Your account and how you sign in
  • Your bank connections, which Flo also revokes at Plaid, and everything your banks and Apple Wallet sent
  • Your budget, categories, notes and everything else you entered

It's deleted within 30 days, and our backups age out within a further 30 days.

What's kept

Nothing in Flo. Records of a payment stay with whoever handled it (Apple, Google Play or Stripe) under their own policies, and our hosting providers keep standard security logs, including IP addresses, for a limited time. The privacy policy has the details.

Deleting some of your data

You don't have to delete your account to remove a bank's data. In Settings, disconnecting a bank lets you delete everything it brought in, right away. Transactions you typed in yourself can be deleted one at a time from Transactions.
