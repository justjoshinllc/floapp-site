What Flo is, and isn't

Flo is a personal cash-flow forecasting tool. It shows projections based on the bills, income, and account activity you provide or connect through a third party. Please note that projections are estimates and can be wrong: a bill can post early, a paycheck late, and an amount can differ. Flo is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice, and we're not a bank, broker, or adviser. Flo shows investment and retirement balances for tracking only and doesn't recommend investments. Decisions about your money are yours; check your bank before you rely on a number.

Forecasts, reminders and estimates

Everything Flo shows about the future (safe to spend, projected balances, card payment forecasts, savings pace, and the notifications that warn you about a dip or a payment due) is an estimate built from your plan, your bank activity and any figures you have entered. A card's forecast is your estimate (or Flo's) until a statement closes, when the statement's real total takes over. The value Flo shows for something you own, such as a home or a vehicle, is a rough estimate from public price indexes, not an appraisal. Estimates can be wrong or late, notifications can be delayed or not arrive, and none of it replaces checking your bank and your statements. You're responsible for paying your bills on time.

Smarter sorting

Suggestions can be wrong; nothing is applied until you accept it, and you can turn the feature off at any time.
