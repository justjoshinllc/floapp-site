Questions

How does Flo connect to my accounts?

Through Plaid, read-only. On iPhone, Apple Card, Apple Cash and Apple Savings link from Wallet. Flo never sees your bank login. Disconnect anything in Settings.

Does Flo file transactions for me?

No. Flo shows its best guess; you decide, one tap per place. No automatic rules.

Can Flo move my money?

No. Read-only by design.

Does Flo use AI?

Only if you turn on Smarter sorting. It sends business names, categories, amounts and how often. Never your email, account numbers, balances or payments to people. Anthropic's Claude, which doesn't train on it.

What do notifications say?

Three, all optional: heading under your floor in the next two weeks, a card payment due in three days, a bank that stopped syncing. None shows an amount.

What does it cost?

Tracking by hand is free. Linking your banks is $5.99 a month, and one subscription covers your whole household.

When is it available?

Now, on the web at app.floapp.app. The iPhone app is in beta on TestFlight.
