Where your money is going, not just where it went

Most budgeting apps report the past. Flo looks ahead, at every bill, paycheck and card payment still to come, so you can see a problem weeks before it lands.

Questions

How does Flo connect to my accounts?

For banks and credit unions, through Plaid, with a read-only connection to balances and transactions. On iPhone, you can also link Apple Card, Apple Cash and Apple Savings from Apple Wallet. Flo never sees your bank login, and you can disconnect anything in Settings.

Which accounts can I link?

Checking, savings, credit cards, loans and investment accounts (a 401k, an IRA, a brokerage account, an HSA) at any US bank, credit union or brokerage Plaid supports, plus Apple Card, Apple Cash and Apple Savings on iPhone. Investment accounts are never counted as cash to spend: Flo shows what you put in and what the market did.

Can Flo move my money?

No. Flo is read-only by design. It can't transfer, pay, or withdraw anything.

Does Flo use AI?

Only if you turn on Smarter sorting. It sends business names, categories, amounts and how often, never your email, account numbers, balances, transfers or payments to people, and your name is removed first. It uses Anthropic's Claude, which doesn't train on it.

What do notifications say?

Three kinds, all optional: your balance heading below your floor in the next two weeks, a card payment due in three days, and a bank that stopped syncing. None shows an amount.

What does it cost?

Free during the beta. At launch, linking accounts will be part of a paid plan; tracking by hand stays free.

When is it available?

Flo Forecast is in beta on TestFlight. Join the early-access list on the support page to get an invitation.

Can I use Flo on a computer?

Yes. Sign in at app.floapp.app with the same account you use on iPhone.

Can I share Flo with a partner?

Yes. Set up a household and invite them. You share one budget, and one subscription covers every bank in it.
