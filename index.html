Cash-flow forecasting for iPhone

Know what's safe to spend — and what's coming.

Flo links your checking, savings and credit cards, learns your bills and paychecks, and projects your balance forward day by day. One number tells you what you can spend without a bill bouncing later — and Flo gives you a heads-up two weeks before a dip. Coming to the App Store as Flo Forecast.

Join the early access list How it works

Read-only. Flo never moves money and never sees your bank password.

Where your money is going, not just where it went

Most budgeting apps report the past. Flo looks ahead — at every bill, paycheck and card payment still to come — so you can see a problem weeks before it lands.

Safe to spend

The lowest your balance is projected to reach, minus a floor you set — into next month, over three months, or the year. The chart starts on the first day you'd dip below it, so you see when, not just how much.

A heads-up before it happens

If a sync shows your balance dipping below your floor in the next two weeks, Flo tells you. Card payments get a reminder three days out. Notifications never show an amount on your lock screen.

Credit cards that make sense

Floating spend is everything on your cards that hasn't been paid yet, grouped by the month each bill is due and checked against what you budgeted — so the payment never catches you off guard.

A budget that knows your month

One plan of bills, income and spending money. Page through any month and see what's happened so far, what's planned, and where it's projected to end up. Charges nothing in the budget covers are called out.

Sorting that learns

New transactions land in To sort, matched to a bill or a budget line when they fit. File a place once and Flo remembers; split a single charge across categories when a store run was two things.

Savings with a finish line

Give a savings account a goal and a date, and Flo shows the climb month by month — on pace or behind, and by how much.

Insights you can tap into

Spending by category as a donut and month-by-month bars, money in vs. out, net by month, and how your plan compares with what really happens. Tap a category to see every charge behind it.

Smarter sorting, if you want it

Optional AI help naming unfamiliar businesses and suggesting a budget. It's off until you say yes, never sees account numbers or balances, removes your name first, and every suggestion waits for you.

Built for the question you ask at the register

Flo isn't a net-worth dashboard or an envelope system. It answers one thing well: can I afford this without causing a problem later?

Questions

How does Flo connect to my bank?

Through Plaid, the same service used by most banking and budgeting apps. You sign in on Plaid's screen, not ours; Flo receives a read-only connection to balances and transactions and never sees your credentials. Link as many accounts as you like — checking, savings and credit cards — and disconnect any of them from Settings.

Can Flo move my money?

No. Flo is read-only by design. It can't transfer, pay, or withdraw anything.

Does Flo use AI?

Only if you turn on Smarter sorting, and Flo asks before it does. It sends a business name and category as your bank wrote them, amounts, how often, and your category names — never your email, account numbers or balances, payments to or from people, or transfers, and your name is removed first. It uses Anthropic's Claude, which doesn't train on it. Every suggestion waits for you to accept it.

What do notifications say?

Two kinds, both optional: a heads-up when a sync shows your balance dipping below your floor in the next two weeks, and a reminder three days before a card payment is due. Neither ever shows an amount.

What does it cost?

Early access is free while Flo Forecast is in TestFlight. When it launches, linking a bank will be part of a paid plan because each connection costs us money; tracking by hand will stay free.

When is it available?

Flo Forecast is in development for iPhone. Join the early-access list on the support page and you'll get a TestFlight invitation when the beta opens.
