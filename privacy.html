How Flo handles your data

Effective September 22, 2026 · Updated September 25, 2026 · Applies to floapp.app, floforecast.com and the Flo Forecast app

This policy explains what JustJoshin LLC ("we", "us") collects when you use Flo, why, and what control you have over it. It's written to be read, not skimmed; if anything is unclear, email support@floapp.app.

The short version

  • We collect the minimum needed to forecast your cash flow: an email address to sign you in, and, only if you choose to link a bank, your account balance and transactions, read-only, through Plaid.
  • We never see or store your bank username or password. Plaid handles that on its own screen.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • If you join the early-access list on this website, we keep your email only to send your beta invitation.
  • You can disconnect your bank or delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

This website

floapp.app and floforecast.com are static pages. They set no cookies and run no analytics. They collect two things, and only if you choose to give them:

  • The early-access list. If you join it, we store your email address, the page you signed up from, and when. We use it only to send you a TestFlight invitation and, when Flo launches, one note that it's available. We don't add you to a newsletter, and we don't share or sell the list. The list is stored in Supabase in the United States, which keeps standard request logs (including IP address) for security. We delete the list once Flo is on the App Store and that note has gone out, or your email sooner if you ask at support@floapp.app.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep the message so we can reply.

The app: what we collect and why

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address and, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides (which may be a private relay address). We use it to sign you in, send verification and password-reset messages, and contact you about your account. We don't send marketing email.

Bank data, if you link an account. Linking is optional. If you do, you sign in to your bank on Plaid's screen; Plaid gives us a read-only token that lets us retrieve your account name, balance, and transaction history (date, description, amount, pending status). We use this to build your forecast, match transactions to the bills and income you've set up, and show you your activity. We cannot initiate payments, transfers, or withdrawals. Plaid's own handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

What you enter. Bills, income, categories, groups, one-off items, notes, your balance floor, and, if you track by hand instead of linking a bank, the balance and transactions you type in.

Smarter sorting (optional AI). Off unless you turn it on, and Flo asks first. When it's on, to name unfamiliar businesses and suggest categories and budget amounts, Flo sends Anthropic (the maker of Claude) the business name and category as your bank wrote them, amounts, how often a charge repeats, and your category names. It never sends your email, account numbers or balances, payments to or from people, or transfers, and your name is removed first if your sign-in shared it. Anthropic doesn't train its models on this data and normally deletes it within 30 days. Every suggestion waits for you to accept it. You can turn Smarter sorting off at any time in Settings.

Notifications, if you allow them. If you turn on notifications, we store your phone's push token (an address for delivering notifications to that device, not a phone number or identifier of you) so our server can send them. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple Push Notification service. They say that something needs a look or that a card payment is due, and never include an amount. Turning notifications off in Settings removes the token.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, a report is sent to Sentry so we can fix it: the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, your device model and OS version, and an opaque account identifier so we can tell two crashes apart without knowing whose they are. It does not include your name, your email, your IP address, or anything from your accounts. Before anything is sent, the text of the report is stripped of anything shaped like an amount of money, an email address, a long number, or an access token — because the wording of an error is written by whatever failed, and can carry those without anyone having intended it. Flo records no analytics, no screen recordings, and no session replay. You can turn crash reports off in Settings, and nothing is sent from then on.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't use your data to train machine-learning models.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Where your data lives and who can reach it

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by database-level rules so that each user's data is readable only by that user's authenticated session. Plaid access tokens are kept in a separate, further-restricted store the app itself cannot read. A small number of service providers process data on our behalf solely to run Flo: Supabase (hosting and authentication), Plaid (bank connectivity), Apple (app distribution, Sign in with Apple and notification delivery), Expo (notification delivery), Anthropic (Smarter sorting, only if you turn it on), an email provider for sign-in and account messages, and Sentry (crash reports). Each is bound by its own terms to use the data only to provide that service.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we revoke the Plaid connection and delete your account record, bank data, and everything you entered within 30 days; backups age out within a further 30 days. If you disconnect a bank without deleting your account, the transactions already imported stay in your account until you delete them or the account.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect a bank at any time from Settings.
  • Turn Smarter sorting or notifications off at any time from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings; this is permanent.
  • Export or correct your data, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing support@floapp.app. We'll respond within 30 days and won't charge for it.

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have the rights those laws provide, including to know, delete, and correct; we honor those requests for everyone, wherever you live, and we don't discriminate for exercising them. We don't "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Children

Flo is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will ask you to acknowledge the updated policy before you carry on, and we'll update the effective date above. Earlier versions are available on request.
